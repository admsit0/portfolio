A more personal page

Beyond the CV.

This is the longer version of the home page: what I work on, why, and what I would like to do next. The CV, projects and results are in the technical portfolio.

01

Looking inside models

Both of my theses ask what happens inside a network, not only at its output. In the bachelor's thesis that meant measuring entropy and activation states in CNNs trained with seven different regularisers. In the MSc thesis it means looking for deceptive behaviour in the latent representations of an LLM. If a model can deceive, I would rather find where that happens and change it than only filter what it says.

02

Why Atlas exists

Reading about an algorithm and understanding its decisions are not the same thing. So instead of another set of notes, Atlas lets you change a search heuristic, see which nodes an algorithm visits, or follow a gradient through a small network. It covers symbolic AI, deep learning and generative AI, in English and Spanish, and I keep adding to it.

03

Organising for others

I helped found GDG on Campus UAM, kept its accounts as treasurer, and have been its president since July 2026. Most of the work is not on stage: finding rooms and speakers, coordinating teams of six to thirteen people, preparing a Git workshop twice with Ibón de Mingo, or organising our first machine learning contest, and writing its guide, with Ibón and Héctor Tablero.

04

What comes next

In 2027 I finish the MSc. After that I want to work as an AI engineer on making language models more reliable, close enough to research to keep asking why a model does what it does. I also want to keep explaining things: Atlas and GDG UAM are where I practise that.

05

Further down the road

One day I want to start a non-profit. It does not have a name or a date yet, and I would rather not describe it before it is real. What I do have is practice in the unglamorous part of making something exist for other people, and that is what I want to bring to it.
